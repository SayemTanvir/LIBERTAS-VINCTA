LIBERTAS VINCTA - COMPLETE GAMEPLAY GUIDE
==========================================

This is the complete spoiler guide for the current game.
It covers controls, rooms, interactions, key and letter order, enemy rules,
all Part I routes, Part II branches, Ley Nexus access, and every ending.


1. BASIC CONTROLS
-----------------
W A S D / Arrow Keys : Move
Shift                 : Sprint
Ctrl                  : Crouch / move quietly
B                     : Hold breath
F                     : Toggle flashlight
E                     : Interact, collect, read, hide, solve, or use a door
Q                     : Use the selected gadget
R                     : Blood Sigil / branch ability
T                     : Stun Rite when unlocked
Y                     : Blood Trap
H                     : Open the Field Guide
Tab                   : Open inventory and collected letters
Esc                   : Pause or close a modal
C                     : Quick turn


2. GAME START - COLD FOYER
--------------------------
1. Pick up the flashlight.
2. Pick up the Tool Pouch. It gives Els three lockpicks.
3. Try the intro door once. The first attempt only records that it was tested.
4. Return to the door after collecting the required items.
5. Press E to unlock it and enter the Ground Floor.

Power stations:
- Hold the interaction while standing still to recharge the flashlight.
- In Part II, stations also restore HP.
- Moving or taking damage interrupts the session.
- Batteries are required for flashlight charging.


3. INTERACTION TYPES
--------------------
Pickups:
- Flashlight: enables the flashlight.
- Tool Pouch: gives three lockpicks.
- Key: restores one sealed sense to the Hound.
- Letter: provides story clues and unlocks progression.
- Battery: restores flashlight charge.
- Bottle: creates a distant noise distraction.
- Clock: creates a larger noise distraction.
- Knife, Guide Letter, and The Power: required for the Nexus Destroy route.

Puzzles and seals:
- Piano Seal: three steps and one lockpick.
- Vanity Seal: three steps and one lockpick.
- Cracked Ritual Seal: one step and no lockpick.
- Damage or interruption can cancel an active puzzle.

Hiding:
- Els can hide under tables, in wardrobes, beds, toy chests, coat racks,
  and the Nexus ward screens.
- Reusing the same hiding place is dangerous after Memory is restored.
- Press E to leave a hiding place.

Doors and vents:
- Approach the target and press E.
- Locked doors explain their missing requirement instead of opening.
- Vents connect several floors and provide alternate routes.

Lore:
- Assemble the nameplate fragments to reveal VANTREE.
- Some lore events happen automatically and only once per run.
- The Foyer Note warns that the keys concern three stolen senses.


4. HOUND AND SENSORY RULES
--------------------------
Stage 0 - no keys:
- The Hound wanders without Hearing, Sight, or Memory.
- Contact is a non-lethal stagger on this branch.
- Avoid detection to preserve the Untouched route.

After the Hearing Key:
- The Hound investigates noise.
- Two audible pings close together can start an audio hunt.
- Glass, running, and loud surfaces are dangerous.
- Crouch and use carpet or quieter routes.

After the Sight Key:
- The Hound can detect exposure, line of sight, and flashlight use.
- Turn off the flashlight and use real cover.
- Do not cross exposed areas in front of the Hound.

After the Memory Key:
- The Hound remembers used hiding places.
- It can predict routes and revisit previous hiding locations.
- Change both your route and hiding place.

Part II branch behavior:
- Untouched: ordinary senses remain dormant; gadgets are the main defense.
- Vantree: Touch mutation and Blood Magic are active; nearby floor vibration
  can reveal Els.
- Partial Mercy: Hearing and Sight remain active; Memory remains dormant.

Damage or capture leads to checkpoint recovery. A successful attack in Part I
causes capture; Part II attacks reduce HP.


5. KEY AND LETTER ORDER
----------------------
The three keys must be taken in this order:

1. Hearing Key - Ground Floor, Music Room, after the Piano Seal
2. Sight Key   - Upper Floor, Master Bedroom, after the Vanity Seal
3. Memory Key  - Basement, Ritual Chamber, after the Cracked Ritual Seal

Visibility and collection rules:
- The Hearing Key is visible from the beginning.
- It cannot be collected until the Piano Seal is complete.
- After collecting Hearing, Letter I becomes visible.
- After reading Letter I, the Sight Key becomes visible.
- After collecting Sight, Letter II becomes visible.
- After reading Letter II, the Memory Key becomes visible.
- Only the current progression key or letter is visible and collectible.
- Collected keys and letters disappear from the world.

The first two letters give the next key locations:
- Letter I sends Els to the Upper Floor Master Bedroom and the Vanity Seal.
- Letter II sends Els to the Basement Ritual Chamber and the Cracked Seal.

7. PART I ENDINGS
-----------------
There are four Part I results. Three continue to Part II. Three keys create
the Loop instead of a Part II seed.

A. 0 KEYS - UNTOUCHED

Requirements:
- Do not collect any key.
- Do not trigger a detection.

Sequence:
1. Test the Front Door in the Ground Floor Grand Foyer.
2. Without taking a key or being detected, use the Front Door again.
3. The Untouched ending triggers.
4. Continue to begin Part II.

Part II seed:
- The Hound's ordinary senses remain dormant.
- Els receives a full supply of batteries, bottles, and clocks.
- Use Q, crouching, cover, and distractions to survive.

B. 1 KEY - VANTREE

Requirements:
- Collect only the Hearing Key.
- Read at least four estate letters.
- Do not collect Sight or Memory.

Sequence:
1. Complete the three Piano Seal steps.
2. Collect the Hearing Key.
3. Read Letter I.
4. Go upstairs and complete the Vanity Seal, but do not collect Sight.
5. Read Letter II and collect enough additional estate letters.
6. Go to the Basement after reaching four estate letters.
7. Use the Ritual Conduit in BS-09.
8. Continue from the Vantree ending into Part II.

Part II seed:
- Touch mutation is active.
- Blood Magic is available.
- Maximum HP is 80.
- Nearby floor vibration can reveal Els.

C. 2 KEYS - PARTIAL MERCY

Requirements:
- Collect Hearing and Sight.
- Do not collect Memory.

Sequence:
1. Complete the Piano Seal and collect Hearing.
2. Read Letter I.
3. Complete the Vanity Seal and collect Sight.
4. Go to the Flooded Cellar in BS-04.
5. Use the Maintenance Exit.
6. Continue from Partial Mercy into Part II.

Part II seed:
- Hearing and Sight remain active.
- Memory remains dormant.
- Partial Sigil is available.

D. 3 KEYS - LOOP

Sequence:
1. Collect Hearing.
2. Read Letter I.
3. Collect Sight.
4. Read Letter II.
5. Collect Memory.
6. Return to the Ground Floor Front Door.
7. Trigger the Loop.

Loop result:
- Els restarts at the Ground Floor.
- Keys, letters, ordinary inventory, and hiding history reset.
- No Part II seed is created.
- The second Loop unlocks a Memory Fragment.
- The three-key route is not a final victory route.


8. HOW TO REACH THE LEY NEXUS WITH EACH COMBINATION
-----------------------------------------------------
0-key route:
1. Finish Untouched at the Ground Floor Front Door.
2. Press Continue to start Part II in Roots.
3. Travel through CR-01, CR-02, CR-03, CR-04, and CR-05.
4. Cross CR-06 Echo Threshold into the Chamber of Echoes.
5. Successfully use the current branch mechanic three times.
6. Go to CE-05 and use the Nexus Descent.

1-key route:
1. Take only Hearing and read Letters I-IV.
2. Use the BS-09 Ritual Conduit for the Vantree ending.
3. Press Continue to start in Roots.
4. Travel through the Roots to CR-06 and the Chamber of Echoes.
5. Use Blood Magic or the current branch mechanic three times.
6. Go to CE-05 and use the Nexus Descent.

2-key route:
1. Take Hearing, read Letter I, then take Sight.
2. Use the BS-04 Flooded Cellar Maintenance Exit.
3. Press Continue to start in Roots.
4. Travel to the Chamber of Echoes.
5. Use the Partial Mercy branch mechanic three times.
6. Go to CE-05 and use the Nexus Descent.

3-key route:
1. Take Hearing, Sight, and Memory.
2. Use the Ground Floor Front Door to trigger the Loop.
3. This route does not reach the Nexus directly.
4. After the reset, choose the 0-key, 1-key, or 2-key route to reach Part II.

Common gate:
- The Nexus Descent opens after three successful branch mechanic uses.
- The Descent is in CE-05, after the Chamber of Echoes.
- Entering through the Descent loads the wide Ley Nexus arena.


9. LEY NEXUS: HOW TO FIND THE GUIDE LETTER
--------------------------------------------
The Guide Letter is inside the Ley Nexus. You cannot collect it before entering
the Nexus through CE-05's Nexus Descent.

Follow this exact path after entering the Nexus:

1. Start exploring the wide arena.
2. Find LN-A, the Destroy rune, in the western/left section.
3. Move right from LN-A and collect the Knife.
4. Continue right toward the middle of the arena.
5. The Guide Letter is after the Knife and before LN-B, the Flee rune.
6. Press E on the Guide Letter and read it completely.
7. Reading the Guide Letter reveals The Power at the far side of the arena.
8. Travel to The Power, collect it, and return to LN-A for Destroy.

Landmark order from the Nexus entrance:

LN-A Destroy rune -> Knife -> Guide Letter -> LN-B Flee rune -> LN-C Remain rune

The Power is revealed only after the Guide Letter is read. If it is not visible,
the letter has not been successfully read yet.


10. LEY NEXUS ARENA AND HOUND
-----------------------------
The Nexus is a wide free-roam arena with three distant runes, two hiding screens,
a cyan power/heal station, and the Destroy-route items.

The Hound:
- Starts by patrolling the entire arena.
- Does not camp permanently near a rune.
- Returns to roaming after losing the player.
- Keeps its existing Hearing, Sight, Touch, and Memory systems.

Rune alarm:
- Pressing E at any rune for the first time starts the red alarm.
- The alarm immediately gives the Hound the player's trail.
- The Hound begins actively hunting for the rest of the sequence.
- Hiding screens and rubble routes can break line of sight.

Rune hold rules:
- LN-B and LN-C require an uninterrupted 20-second hold of E.
- Movement, damage, detection, or releasing E resets the hold.
- Pausing freezes the timer.
- LN-A starts the alarm but cannot finish Destroy without the item chain.


11. NEXUS ENDINGS
-----------------
A. DESTROY - TRUE VICTORY

Required sequence:
1. Collect the Knife.
2. Read the Guide Letter.
3. Collect The Power after it becomes visible.
4. Return to LN-A and press E once.
5. Let the alarmed Hound approach.
6. Get within approximately 192 pixels and press Y.
7. Blood Trap costs exactly 40 HP and holds the Hound for 18 seconds.
8. Move beside the trapped Hound.
9. Press E on the trap prompt to set the trap and drop The Power.
10. The Power is consumed and the Hound dies.
11. An ending door appears.
12. Press E at the ending door.

Destroy requirements:
- Knife in inventory
- Guide Letter read
- The Power in inventory
- Hound currently bound by Blood Trap
- Clear line of sight to the Hound

B. FLEE

1. Go to LN-B.
2. Hold E for 20 seconds.
3. Do not move, take damage, or become detected.
4. Enter the door that appears.
5. Els escapes and the Flee narration plays.

Knife, Guide Letter, and The Power are not required for Flee.

C. REMAIN / RESTART

1. Go to LN-C.
2. Hold E for 20 seconds.
3. Avoid movement, damage, and detection.
4. Enter the door that appears.
5. The game returns to the Ground Floor through the Loop reset system.

Remain is a restart outcome, not a final victory. Current keys, letters,
inventory, and Nexus progress are cleared.


12. Q GADGET PRIORITY
---------------------
Q checks items in this order:

1. Battery - only when flashlight charge is below approximately 56 percent
2. The Power - during the Nexus Destroy sequence
3. Bottle
4. Clock

The Power has priority over bottles and clocks. It can only complete Destroy
through the trapped-Hound interaction prompt.


13. ABILITIES AND FAILED USES
-----------------------------
Blood Sigil:
- Available on the Blood Magic or partial-magic branches.
- Creates a temporary suppression circle.
- Failed range or unavailable casts spend nothing.

Stun Rite:
- Available when the Blood Magic branch unlocks it.
- Requires the correct branch and cooldown state.
- Failed casts spend nothing.

Blood Trap:
- Available in the Nexus after reading the Guide Letter.
- Key: Y.
- Range: approximately 192 pixels.
- Cost: 40 HP flat.
- Duration: 18 seconds.
- Failed range, line-of-sight, cooldown, or low-HP checks spend nothing.
- Blood Trap cannot reduce the caster to zero HP.


14. SURVIVAL RULES
------------------
- Sprinting creates more noise and drains the flashlight faster.
- Crouching reduces noise but does not fully defeat Touch mutation.
- Flashlight use is risky after Sight is restored.
- Bottles and clocks should be thrown away from the route you intend to use.
- Do not reuse the same hiding place after Memory is active.
- Approach doors, puzzles, and stations before pressing E.
- Damage can interrupt puzzles, charging, and anchor holds.
- HP reaching zero causes death/capture and checkpoint recovery.
- Save/Continue restores zone, position, keys, letters, HP, flashlight,
  inventory, and progression state.
